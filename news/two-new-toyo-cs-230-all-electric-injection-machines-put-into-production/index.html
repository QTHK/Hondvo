Two New TOYO CS-230 All-Electric Injection Machines Put into Production

2026.06.15

To meet the growing order demands from medical device clients, the company has introduced two new Japanese TOYO CS-230 all-electric injection machines, now officially operating in the Class 100,000 c…

To meet growing order volumes from medical device customers, HONDVO has installed two new TOYO CS-230 all-electric injection molding machines. Both machines are now in production inside the Class 100,000 cleanroom.

All-electric molding brings lower contamination risk, higher repeatability and better energy efficiency compared with hydraulic equipment. The new machines will be dedicated to high-precision components such as infusion parts, syringe components and IVD consumables, where dimensional stability and clean appearance are critical.

Each machine has been qualified under HONDVO's IQ/OQ/PQ program before release, with installation records, process window definition and three-batch performance validation archived for traceability.
