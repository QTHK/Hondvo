News/Swiss Hauser S35 Jig Grinder Installation & Commissioning Completed

The Swiss-made Hauser S35 jig grinder has completed installation and commissioning at HONDVO Tooling. The machine extends the company&#39;s capability in high-precision cavity finishing and post-machining correction.

With positioning accuracy at the micron level, the Hauser S35 is used for critical mold components where cavity tolerance and surface finish directly affect the quality of molded medical parts. It strengthens HONDVO&#39;s in-house ability to handle complex core inserts, electrode fitting and fine surface correction without outsourcing.

The equipment was accepted against agreed acceptance criteria and entered the preventive maintenance and calibration schedule from day one.

瑞士产 Hauser S35 坐标磨床已在弘欧模具完成安装调试，进一步提升了公司在高精度型腔精加工与后工序修正方面的能力。

该设备定位精度达到微米级，主要用于型腔公差与表面质量直接影响注塑件品质的关键模具零件，使弘欧能够在内部完成复杂型芯镶件、电极配作与精细表面修正，无需外协。

设备已按既定验收标准完成验收，并自投入使用起纳入预防性维护与校准计划。
