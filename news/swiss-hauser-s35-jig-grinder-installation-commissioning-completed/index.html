Swiss Hauser S35 Jig Grinder Installation & Commissioning Completed

2026.04.22

The Hauser S35 jig grinder with 0.001mm precision has completed installation and commissioning in the mold workshop, further enhancing precision mold manufacturing capabilities.

The Swiss-made Hauser S35 jig grinder has completed installation and commissioning at HONDVO Tooling. The machine extends the company's capability in high-precision cavity finishing and post-machining correction.

With positioning accuracy at the micron level, the Hauser S35 is used for critical mold components where cavity tolerance and surface finish directly affect the quality of molded medical parts. It strengthens HONDVO's in-house ability to handle complex core inserts, electrode fitting and fine surface correction without outsourcing.

The equipment was accepted against agreed acceptance criteria and entered the preventive maintenance and calibration schedule from day one.
