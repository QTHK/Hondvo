HONDVO Technology Passed ISO 13485 Annual Surveillance Audit

2026-07-28

News/HONDVO Technology Passed ISO 13485 Annual Surveillance Audit

HONDVO Technology (Dongguan) Co., Ltd. has successfully passed the annual surveillance audit of its ISO 13485 medical device quality management system. The audit covered the full injection molding production process, including raw material incoming inspection, process control, cleanroom environment monitoring, traceability and final release.

The auditor reviewed documented procedures, production records and calibration files, and conducted on-site interviews with process owners. No major non-conformities were raised. The result confirms that HONDVO&#39;s quality system continues to meet the requirements of ISO 13485 and remains ready to support medical device customers in regulated markets.

HONDVO will continue to invest in process validation, Cpk/Ppk capability monitoring and operator training to keep the system effective and audit-ready at all times.

弘欧科技（东莞）有限公司顺利通过 ISO 13485 医疗器械质量管理体系年度监督审核。本次审核覆盖注塑生产全过程，包括原材料来料检验、过程控制、洁净环境监测、追溯管理与成品放行。

审核组查阅了程序文件、生产记录与校准档案，并与各工序负责人进行了现场访谈，未发现严重不符合项。审核结果证明弘欧的质量体系持续满足 ISO 13485 要求，能够稳定支持受监管市场的医疗器械客户。

弘欧将持续投入工艺验证、Cpk/Ppk 过程能力监控与人员培训，确保体系长期有效、随时可接受审核。
