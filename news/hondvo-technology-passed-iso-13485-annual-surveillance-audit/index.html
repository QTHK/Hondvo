HONDVO Technology Passed ISO 13485 Annual Surveillance Audit

2026.07.28

HONDVO Technology has successfully passed the ISO 13485 medical device quality management system annual surveillance audit, covering the entire injection molding production process.

HONDVO Technology (Dongguan) Co., Ltd. has successfully passed the annual surveillance audit of its ISO 13485 medical device quality management system. The audit covered the full injection molding production process, including raw material incoming inspection, process control, cleanroom environment monitoring, traceability and final release.

The auditor reviewed documented procedures, production records and calibration files, and conducted on-site interviews with process owners. No major non-conformities were raised. The result confirms that HONDVO's quality system continues to meet the requirements of ISO 13485 and remains ready to support medical device customers in regulated markets.

HONDVO will continue to invest in process validation, Cpk/Ppk capability monitoring and operator training to keep the system effective and audit-ready at all times.
