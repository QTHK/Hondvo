HONDVO TOOLING Passed IATF 16949 Recertification Audit

2026.03.08

HONDVO TOOLING successfully passed the IATF 16949 automotive quality management system recertification audit. System effectiveness was highly praised by auditors, continuing to meet stringent automot…

HONDVO Tooling successfully passed the recertification audit of its automotive quality management system. The audit confirmed the effectiveness of the company's process controls, change management and continuous improvement mechanisms.

Although HONDVO's website now focuses on medical device manufacturing, the automotive-grade disciplines developed through IATF 16949 — structured problem solving, layered process audits and rigorous traceability — continue to benefit medical mold projects. These practices are applied to tooling design reviews, machining process control and final mold validation.

The recertification is an internal capability milestone rather than a change in the website's stated scope, which remains centered on Class II medical device components and precision molds.
