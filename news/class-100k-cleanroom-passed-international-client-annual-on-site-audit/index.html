Class 100K Cleanroom Passed International Client Annual On-Site Audit

2026.02.20

The Class 100,000 cleanroom successfully passed the annual on-site audit by a European medical device client, covering cleanliness control, SOP compliance, and batch traceability. Achieved excellent …

HONDVO's Class 100,000 injection molding cleanroom passed the annual on-site audit conducted by an international medical device client.

The client reviewed environmental monitoring records, personnel and material flow, cleaning and sanitation procedures, and in-process controls. Particular attention was paid to particle counts, temperature and humidity trends, and the separation of molded parts from non-clean areas.

No major findings were raised. The audit outcome supports continued supply of cleanroom-molded components for the client's regulated product lines and reflects HONDVO's commitment to maintaining a controlled production environment.
