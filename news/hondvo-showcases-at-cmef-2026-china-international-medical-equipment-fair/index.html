HONDVO Showcases at CMEF 2026 China International Medical Equipment Fair

2026.05.10

HONDVO Technology showcased its core products including enteral nutrition infusion systems and injection drug delivery systems at CMEF 2026, reaching cooperation intentions with multiple domestic and…

HONDVO Technology and HONDVO Tooling showcased their medical molding capabilities at CMEF 2026, one of the largest medical equipment exhibitions in Asia.

The booth highlighted precision injection components for infusion therapy, enteral nutrition, IVD sampling and respiratory care, together with examples of medical-grade tooling. Visitors from Europe, North America and Southeast Asia showed particular interest in HONDVO's ability to combine mold development and volume molding under one quality system.

The event provided valuable face-to-face time with existing partners and new prospects. Discussions focused on design-for-manufacturing support, regulatory documentation and long-term capacity planning for 2027.
