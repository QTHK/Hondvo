New German RODERS High-Speed Machining Center Delivered to Mold Center

2026-01-12

News/New German RODERS High-Speed Machining Center Delivered to Mold Center

A new German-made RODERS high-speed machining center has been delivered to HONDVO&#39;s mold center, expanding capacity for precision cavity machining and high-finish surface work.

High-speed machining allows finer surface finishes and shorter cycle times on complex mold geometries. Combined with the center&#39;s existing EDM and CMM equipment, the new machine shortens the route from rough machining to finished, inspected cavity.

The addition supports both medical mold projects and high-precision inserts used in volume injection molding, giving customers shorter lead times without relaxing dimensional or surface requirements.

一台德国产 RODERS 高速加工中心已交付弘欧模具中心，进一步扩充了精密型腔加工与高光洁度表面处理能力。

高速加工能够在复杂模具几何上实现更细的表面粗糙度与更短的循环时间。配合模具中心现有的电火花与三坐标测量设备，新设备缩短了从粗加工到成品型腔检验的工艺路径。

该设备的加入同时服务于医疗模具项目与批量注塑所需的高精度镶件，帮助客户在不降低尺寸与表面要求的前提下缩短交期。
