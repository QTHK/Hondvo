New German RODERS High-Speed Machining Center Delivered to Mold Center

2026.01.12

Following the Hauser S35 jig grinder, the mold center adds another major equipment — a German RODERS RXP 500 DCS high-speed machining center has completed installation and acceptance, achieving dual …

A new German-made RODERS high-speed machining center has been delivered to HONDVO's mold center, expanding capacity for precision cavity machining and high-finish surface work. High-speed machining allows finer surface finishes and shorter cycle times on complex mold geometries. Combined with the center's existing EDM and CMM equipment, the new machine shortens the route from rough machining to finished, inspected cavity. The addition supports both medical mold projects and high-precision inserts used in volume injection molding, giving customers shorter lead times without relaxing dimensional or surface requirements.
