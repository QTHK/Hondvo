                                        HONDVO

                                        HONDVO Technology (Dongguan) Co., Ltd.

                                        Leveraging the strong manufacturing capabilities of HONDVO TOOLING, we undertake R&D and mass production of a wide range of high-precision injection-molded products. Equipped with 21 injection molding machines — including SUMITOMO all-electric, TOYO hydraulic, and dedicated LSR (liquid silicone rubber) models — we deliver everything from precision structural parts, transparent optical components, and overmolded parts to liquid silicone parts in a one-stop process.

                                        We are currently focused on the medical device sector, with our main products including:

                                        IVD ConsumablesReaction cups, reagent strips, microfluidic chips, cuvettes, and other precision micro-molded components
                                        Drug Delivery DevicesInsulin pen components, pre-filled syringe plungers, spray pumps, and valve assemblies
                                        Surgical Instrument PartsDisposable surgical handles, trocar assemblies, and stapler structural components
                                        Medical Electronics HousingsHousings and panels for medical devices such as monitors, infusion pumps, and portable diagnostic equipment
                                        High-Value Overmolded ComponentsMetal-insert molding, LSR seals, medical catheter connectors, and Luer fittings

                                        Our production environment features two Class 100,000 cleanrooms, with full support for IQ/OQ/PQ validation services, and strictly follows the ISO 13485 quality management system.

                                        In parallel, we also accept industrial-grade injection orders for intelligent robot structural parts and automotive functional parts, enabling concurrent multi-sector delivery.

                                        HONDVO TOOLING LIMITED (Dongguan) Co., Ltd.

                                        We specialize in the design, manufacturing, and delivery of ultra-precision injection molds. With years of deep expertise in the mold industry, we provide end-to-end capabilities — from product structure review and mold design to precision machining and trial molding. Our full-brand equipment matrix ranges from Swiss Hauser jig grinders and German RODERS 5-axis CNC to Japanese YASDA high-speed machining centers, covering complete mold manufacturing processes including CNC machining, EDM, wire-cutting, and precision grinding. Mold accuracy reaches micron level, serving high-end manufacturing fields such as medical devices, intelligent robotics, and automotive components.

                                        IQ/OQ/PQ Validation System

                                        Strictly following ISO 13485 and FDA 21 CFR Part 820, we maintain a standardized IQ/OQ/PQ qualification system. Through installation, operational and performance qualification, we ensure compliant equipment, a stable process window and consistent mass-production quality.

                                        IQ

                                        Installation Qualification

                                        • Verify equipment model, configuration and installation accuracy
                                        • Validate energy supply, cleanroom systems and mold fitment
                                        • Archive complete installation documentation for medical admission compliance
                                        OQ

                                        Operational Qualification

                                        • Limit-parameter, multi-batch material and working-condition adaptation tests
                                        • Lock in the optimal stable process window
                                        • Cleanroom dynamic-environment validation with standardized SOP and deviation handling
                                        PQ

                                        Performance Qualification

                                        • ≥3-batch continuous production validation
                                        • Cpk/Ppk process capability data analysis
                                        • Full data retention with auditability and traceability; re-validation after any change

                                        We operate a complete traceability and batch control system fully aligned with ISO 13485:2016. Every incoming lot arrives with the supplier's Certificate of Analysis (one lot, one certificate) and receives its own batch number under our Batch Number Management System (WI-QA-007). All incoming material passes IQC inspection and release per our Product Identification and Traceability Procedure (HO-2-011), with non-conforming material quarantined under our Nonconforming Control Procedure (HO-2-016). We apply First-In, First-Out (FIFO) storage and release, and each production batch is logged together with raw-material batch, machine, mold, operator and process parameters — one production batch always uses one raw-material batch. This gives 100% traceability in both directions: forward from raw material to finished product to customer, and backward from a finished product or complaint to the raw-material supplier. We run a traceability drill at least once a year, confirming we can retrieve all records of a randomly selected batch within 4 hours. Batch records are kept for one year after product shelf-life expiry, or at least 10 years, whichever is longer.
