About Us

The group operates through two entities — one for injection moulding and one for mould design and manufacturing — supported by cleanroom capacity and a 125-person team.

Two Entities

  • HONDVO Technology (Dongguan) Co., Ltd. — injection moulding.
  • HONDVO TOOLING LIMITED (Dongguan) Co., Ltd. — mould design and manufacturing.

Capabilities

  • 21 injection moulding machines (SUMITOMO all-electric, TOYO hydraulic and dedicated LSR machines).
  • 109 machining units and a 125-person team.
  • Two Class-100,000 cleanrooms.

Core Processes

  • Precision structural parts and transparent optical parts.
  • Over-moulded parts and LSR (liquid silicone rubber) parts.
