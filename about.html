About Us

The group operates through two entities: HONDVO Technology (Dongguan) Co., Ltd. (injection moulding) and HONDVO TOOLING LIMITED (Dongguan) Co., Ltd. (mould design and manufacturing).

Capabilities: 21 injection moulding machines (SUMITOMO all-electric, TOYO hydraulic and dedicated liquid silicone rubber (LSR) machines), 109 machining units, a 125-person team and two Class-100,000 cleanrooms. Processing covers precision structural parts, transparent optical parts, over-moulded parts and LSR parts.
