Products & Services

HONDVO Technology (Dongguan) Co., Ltd. supplies precision injection moulded components for Class II medical devices.

Core product lines: enteral nutrition infusion systems (silicone bottle bodies, moulded caps, diverter connectors, tubing fittings); injection drug delivery systems (high-precision syringe barrels and plunger assemblies); IVD consumables (reaction cups, reagent strips, microfluidic chips, cuvettes); drug delivery devices (insulin pen components, prefilled plungers, spray pumps and valve assemblies); surgical instrument components; medical electronics housings and panels; metal insert moulding, LSR seals, catheter connectors and Luer interfaces.
